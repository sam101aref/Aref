## English (en-US)

**Name:** Arash The Archer

**Short description:** Draw, aim, release. Become the legendary archer who drew Iran's border.

**Full description:**

Long ago, Iran and Turan fought for years. When peace was finally offered, it came with one condition: an Iranian archer would shoot a single arrow, and wherever it fell, there the border would be drawn.

Arash The Archer is a story-driven archery game based on the Persian legend of Arash. Rise from a young archer in a border village to the hero who climbs Mount Damavand to make the greatest shot ever told.

• Turn-based archery duels: pull back, aim and release. Headshots end fights.
• 38 levels in six chapters, from the border village to the forests of Tabaristan, the peace pact and the snowy slopes of Damavand.
• Defend the palisade against raiders, shoot enemy arrows out of the air to protect your companions, and pass the archers' trials.
• Shield bearers, riders, slingers, fire archers, assassins and divs, and four bosses, each fought in several phases.
• Wind that changes every turn.
• Fill your farr meter for special arrows: fire, triple, piercing, the homing Simurgh arrow and the healing haoma arrow.
• Earn coins for new bows, upgrades and outfits.
• The final flight: guide the great arrow from Damavand to the Oxus.
• Fully playable in English and Persian.

No ads. No account needed. Plays offline.

روزگاری دراز، ایران و توران سال‌ها در جنگ بودند. سرانجام صلح پیشنهاد شد، با یک شرط: کمانداری ایرانی یک تیر بیندازد و هر جا فرود آمد، همان‌جا مرز باشد.
